## LES TROIS RÈGLES, PLUS IMPORTANTES QUE LE PROMPT

1. **Aucune coupe.** Un seul mouvement continu, sinon la boucle se voit à chaque tour.
2. **Mouvement très lent.** La caméra bouge à peine. Ce qui bouge, c'est la brume, la lumière,
   un corps qui respire.
3. **8 à 10 secondes, 16:9, sans son.**

**Toujours déposer l'image comme première image** dans Kling, Seedance ou Flow, puis coller le
prompt de mouvement. Le style sera juste du premier coup, puisqu'il part de l'image.

# A · LES VIDÉOS AVEC SILHOUETTES

## S1 — L'arrivée des six → à partir de `video-s1.jpg`
> Animate this image, 10 seconds, one single continuous shot, no cuts.
> The haze drifts very slowly from left to right through the golden beams. The six silhouettes
> breathe almost imperceptibly, standing still. Their long shadows shimmer slightly on the floor.
> The camera pushes in extremely slowly toward them, barely noticeable.
> No faces appear, no one turns around, nothing else moves.

## S2 — Le solo → à partir de `video-s2.jpg`
> Animate this image, 8 seconds, one continuous shot, no cuts.
> The lone silhouette lifts the microphone very slowly toward the mouth and holds it there.
> Haze swirls gently in the golden beam. Dust particles float upward.
> The camera is perfectly still. No face appears, the figure stays a pure black silhouette.

## S3 — Les deux frères → à partir de `video-s3.jpg`
> Animate this image, 8 seconds, one continuous shot, no cuts.
> The two silhouettes stay face to face, motionless, breathing. The golden light between them
> slowly grows brighter, then dims again. Haze drifts across the beam.
> The camera drifts almost imperceptibly to the right. No faces appear, they do not move apart.

## S4 — La traversée → à partir de `video-s4.jpg`
> Animate this image, 10 seconds, one continuous shot, no cuts.
> The silhouette walks very slowly away from the camera toward the distant golden light,
> getting smaller. Thick haze parts around the figure. The floor reflects the light.
> The camera stays perfectly still. No face appears, the figure never turns around.

## S5 — Le salut → à partir de `video-s5.jpg`
> Animate this image, 8 seconds, one continuous shot, no cuts.
> Seen from behind, the six silhouettes slowly join hands, one after the other, from left to
> right, then hold still. The tiny warm lights of the auditorium twinkle faintly.
> The camera is still. No faces appear, no one turns toward the camera.

## S6 — L'attente → à partir de `video-s6.jpg`
> Animate this image, 8 seconds, one continuous shot, no cuts.
> The long shadow on the floor grows slowly longer, as if the person out of frame were coming
> closer, but no one ever enters the frame. Haze drifts slowly. The single golden light flickers
> once, very faintly. The camera is perfectly still. No people visible at any moment.

# B · LES TROIS FONDS ANIMÉS DU SITE

*Ceux-là passent derrière le texte, encore plus discrets.*

## V1 — La brume → `V1-brume.mp4`, à partir de `01-scene.jpg`
> Animate this image with an extremely slow continuous motion, 10 seconds, seamless loop.
> The theatrical haze drifts very slowly from left to right through the golden spotlight beams.
> Tiny gold dust particles rise gently. The beams pulse almost imperceptibly, as if breathing.
> The camera does not move at all. No cuts.
> Everything else stays exactly as in the source image: the logo, the text and the framing are
> unchanged, perfectly sharp, and must not deform.

⚠️ **Si le texte ou le logo ondulent, relance.** S'ils résistent, pars plutôt de
`14-poussiere.jpg` : j'écrirai le titre par-dessus dans le site.

## V2 — Le velours → `V2-velours.mp4`, à partir de `15-velours.jpg`
> Animate this image, 8 seconds, seamless loop, extremely slow.
> A warm golden light sweeps very slowly across the velvet folds, from left to right.
> The fabric moves almost imperceptibly, as if from a faint draught.
> The camera is perfectly still. No cuts, no people, no text.

## V3 — Le passage → `V3-passage.mp4`, sans image de départ
> Cinematic, 8 seconds, no cuts. The camera glides forward through a dark tunnel made of golden
> rings of light floating in black space. The rings pass faster and faster, close to the lens,
> warm gold on deep black, thin haze, fine film grain.
> In the last second the rings dissolve and the image settles into pure black.
> No people, no faces, no text, no logo.

⚠️ **La fin doit être du noir pur** : c'est ce qui permet d'enchaîner sans couture sur le site.
